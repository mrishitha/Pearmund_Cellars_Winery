# Pearmund_Cellars_Winery
### History
Pearmund Cellars Winery, located in Broad Run, Virginia, has been a top winery for over 20 years, earning over 200 gold medals and national acclaim for its 100% Virginia-grown wines. The vineyard, Meriwether, is the oldest Chardonnay vineyard in Virginia, producing wine for over 40 years. Pearmund Cellars offers a variety of wines, including their award-winning Ameritage blend. Chris Pearmund, the founder, has also consulted on numerous winery openings, including Effingham Manor, a sister winery that highlights Virginia's wine history. Pearmund Cellars primarily sells its wines through direct-to-consumer channels, such as wine clubs and tasting rooms, distinguishing it from larger national wineries.

### Problem Statement
Pearmund Cellars and Effingham Manor are operationally separate businesses that share a customer base. Each winery maintains a separate system for marketing, reservations, and Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) sales which makes it difficult to analyze data, spot trends, and market each and between the wineries; a possible solution is to implement a data warehouse. This solution must be cost-effective, easily maintainable, and allow for the easy analysis of consumer trends at each individual winery, as well as between the wineries.  Two cost-effective data warehouses will be explored: Platform as a Service and Software as a Service.  PaaS and SaaS will be prototyped for the wineries, where the effectiveness on analysis and effectiveness in maintainability, will be analyzed; through this analysis it will be determined if a data warehouse is a viable solution.​
